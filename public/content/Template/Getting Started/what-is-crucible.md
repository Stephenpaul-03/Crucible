# What is Crucible?

Crucible is the home I built for documenting my hardware tinkering. It turns my folders of markdown into calm, searchable project pages where I can keep build notes, measurements, diagrams, code, and references connected.

## Why it exists

My hardware projects rarely follow a straight line. I wanted a record of the iterations: what I intended to build, what actually happened on the bench, which assumptions failed, and what I decided to try next.

## The project model

I give every project a sidebar, an overview, and a set of markdown entries grouped by category. Crucible reads those files directly and turns them into the project archive shown here.
