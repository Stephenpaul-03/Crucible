# Crucible Template

This is the structure I use when I start documenting a hardware build in **Crucible**. It gives me a consistent place for my notes, measurements, images, experiments, and decisions.

## What Crucible is

Crucible is my markdown-powered hardware journal. I keep each project in its own workspace with an overview, navigation, build logs, references, and experiments.

## What I document

* My project goals, constraints, parts, and current status.
* The hardware projects I move between on my workbench.
* Tests, measurements, observations, and conclusions.
* Code, diagrams, reference images, and downloadable notes.
* Failures and abandoned approaches that might save me time later.

## Where to begin

I start with a short overview of the build and its current status. From there, I add the design, bill of materials, wiring, firmware, experiments, failures, and references as the project develops. I want the record to show what changed and why—not just where I ended up.
