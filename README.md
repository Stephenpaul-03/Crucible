<p align="center"><strong>A hardware journal for ideas that have not met a soldering iron yet.</strong></p>

Crucible is a hardware journal for projects, experiments, and other excellent opportunities to ask, “Why is that warm?” It keeps the whole journey in one place—especially the bits that mysteriously stop working.

## What it is for

Documenting the gloriously messy path from “this should be easy” to “well, technically it works.” Keep the ideas, measurements, mistakes, fixes, and tiny victories together instead of trusting your future self to remember anything. Bold strategy.

## What goes in it

- Project ideas and goals
- Parts, wiring, code, and reference material
- Build logs, experiments, and measurements
- Failures, fixes, lessons, and next steps

## Why it exists

Because finished projects are lovely, but the route to get there is usually a small documentary about poor decisions. Crucible keeps that story useful, searchable, and ready for the next attempt.

## A good fit for

Electronics builds, embedded experiments, sensor projects, robotics, custom tools, repairs, prototypes, and anything else involving a workbench, a multimeter, and suspicious warmth.

## The rule

Write down what happened before memory turns it into a heroic success story. Record the measurement, keep the failed version, and note what actually fixed the problem. Future you deserves at least one useful breadcrumb.
